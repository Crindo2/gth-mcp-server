Data Coverage

  • Source: SAMHSA National Survey of Substance Abuse Treatment Services (N-SSATS)
  • Scope: Substance abuse (SA-designated) facilities only
  • Levels of care: IOP, Detox, Inpatient Rehab, MAT, Outpatient, PHP
  • Fields: Facility name, address, phone, insurance accepted, services offered, certifications, distance calculations
  • Refresh: Monthly (1st of each month, automated)
